## Purpose and scope

MacSecurify is a local privacy aid intended to discourage casual physical access to selected apps and Safari websites by someone using your Mac. It is not designed to prevent hacker attacks, malware, remote compromise, administrator access, or determined attacks by someone controlling the same macOS account. It is not encryption, a system sandbox, or a replacement for your macOS login password, screen lock, FileVault, backups, or software updates.

## Application protection limits

MacSecurify must be running. Locked apps are hidden and activation is redirected to authentication; their event loops, background work, helpers, audio, and network activity can continue. Launch or activation notifications arrive after macOS begins opening a window, so content may briefly appear. An app that refuses to hide, another viewing route, or system tools can bypass this interaction guard. Low protection requires one method. Medium requires a password plus Touch ID or an enrolled face. High requires a password of at least six characters plus a biometric or an enrolled external USB FIDO2 key. Stronger configured app levels also apply to management actions that can weaken protection. A successful unlock permits normal access for the selected duration. The app deliberately does not freeze or terminate protected apps.

## Safari protection and unlocking

Enable MacSecurify Blocker in Safari → Settings → Extensions. Protection covers new HTTP/HTTPS page loads for selected domains and their subdomains. It does not cover other browsers, previously rendered content, caches, or Safari’s Turn Off Content Blockers control. To open a protected site, open MacSecurify → Websites → Unlock, authenticate, and then reload the blocked tab or use the page opened in Safari. Keep MacSecurify running during temporary access. A forced quit or crash during an unlock can leave the site allowed until the app starts again. Normal quit attempts to restore website locks.

## Authentication and recovery

Choose a password you keep private and enrol only trusted fingerprints on your Mac. Optional webcam face recognition is not a guarantee of identity or liveness. Forgotten passwords can be reset with previously enrolled supported biometrics at Low protection, or a 12-hour local recovery delay. If any app uses Medium or High protection, immediate biometric-only password recovery is disabled; the delay is required and High password resets still require at least six characters. Anyone with access to the Mac can request the delay. Time changes across a reboot can affect this entirely local recovery mechanism. A password reset does not reveal the old password or automatically approve a pending action.
